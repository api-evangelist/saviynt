Access reviews (also called certifications or attestations) are one of the most important controls in an identity security program. They're how organizations catch access that's noncompliant or outlived its purpose before it becomes a breach or audit finding. They're also expensive: every review cycle pulls managers, application owners, and other highly paid leaders away from their actual jobs to certify who has access to what.
