OpenAI and more than 100 organizations recently called for an urgent global effort to strengthen cyber defenses before AI-enabled attacks become more widespread and sophisticated. Their collective cyber defense letter identifies excessive permissions as a systemic weakness and recommends least privilege and stronger access controls as part of the response.
