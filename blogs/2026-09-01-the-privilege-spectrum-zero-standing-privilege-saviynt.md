Executive summary Traditional privileged access management (PAM) was built for a smaller, more predictable set of administrator and technical accounts. Modern enterprises now depend on workforce users, service accounts, cloud workloads and AI agents that can all hold consequential access, with widely different levels of authority, autonomy and potential impact.
